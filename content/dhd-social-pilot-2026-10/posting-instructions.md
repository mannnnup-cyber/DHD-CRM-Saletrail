# Posting instructions

## Preparation

Review every image at full size and every caption before import. Confirm provisional wordmark/colours, sample branding, product availability and wording. Mockups are examples, not stock photos or evidence of completed DHD work. No price or production promise is authorised here.

Use America/Jamaica, UTC−05:00. Proposed dates are 26, 28 and 30 October and 2, 4 and 6 November 2026. Instagram 12:30; Facebook 18:30. These are trial slots chosen for manageable spacing, not proven best times. If stale, flag them and request new times.

Six concepts × two platform variants = twelve draft intents. One JPEG per intent. Alt text is in the manifest. No first comments or hashtags need provider-side features beyond the supplied caption. YouTube is a content-source channel only; this pack does not schedule YouTube posts.

## Import and review

An importer does not currently exist in the inspected Content Studio contract. Implement the work packet before relying on the JSON. A static review can be used immediately without database/provider writes.

Discover the exact connected Instagram/Facebook IDs through authenticated readiness; compare names/platform/workspace with the owner's chosen destinations. Handles in the pack are labels, never authorisation or IDs.

Default action: validate and preview locally. No provider uploads during parse, preview, draft import or reimport. The current upload action is a provider write and is gated; it requires a separate explicit authorised action after release/UAT prerequisites.

Attach each verified uploaded media mapping to its matching draft when authorised. Importing, attaching or changing captions, image bytes, accounts or timing must never approve or schedule. Preserve the existing fresh-revision and owner-review flow.

## Owner publishing review

Show the exact image, caption, destination and Jamaica time. Do not approve placeholders, unknown image hashes or missing mappings. Images must load for review. Reuse the current approval snapshot; do not bypass it for manifest imports. Schedule only after the owner explicitly approves concrete posts and times and the existing publishing gate permits it.

## Results and follow-up

Store per-destination results; a scheduled provider response is not evidence of a live post. Reconcile using existing delivery controls and require native IDs/timestamps for published claims. Never blindly retry unknown/partial results.

Track qualified WhatsApp enquiries, quotations, orders and order value manually against post IDs. A save or like is not a sale. Where customers mention a post, record the evidence rather than infer attribution. After two weeks compare themes, enquiries and actual orders; no numerical targets are invented here.
