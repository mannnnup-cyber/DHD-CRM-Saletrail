# DHD voice

Write plainly, warmly and directly for Jamaican buyers. Use concrete product words: polo, cap, print, stitch, logo, group, quotation. Treat local identity naturally; avoid forced patois or imitation slang. Use one meaningful CTA per post. Keep headlines short and readable on a phone.

Avoid: best in Jamaica, guaranteed results, guaranteed delivery, free/discount offers without approval, instant turnaround, unverified sustainable/eco claims. Do not turn illustrative images or website example stories into customer evidence. No unsolicited direct messages.

Default captions: helpful opening, one useful idea, relevant limitation, one CTA. Instagram quote CTA uses the confirmed WhatsApp number; Facebook can use its direct WhatsApp URL. Educational posts can ask for a save instead of a quote. Supplied captions are recommended drafts, not owner-approved copy.
