# Project Brief

## Project

DHD CRM SalesTrail is a customer relationship management application for Dirty
Hand Designs. It supports sales reps and managers through lead intake, pipeline
tracking, call logging, quoting, invoicing, reporting, and communication
workflows.

## Primary Users

- Sales reps who manage assigned leads, log calls, create follow-up tasks, move
  deals through the pipeline, and contact customers through WhatsApp or email.
- Managers who monitor sales activity, team performance, pipeline health,
  revenue, and operational bottlenecks.
- Administrators or technical operators who configure integrations, environment
  variables, database schemas, deployment, and support workflows.

## Business Goals

- Centralize sales activity for Dirty Hand Designs.
- Reduce lost leads by keeping calls, tasks, deals, quotes, and messages visible.
- Improve manager visibility into team performance and follow-up quality.
- Support Jamaica-specific operational rules such as JMD revenue, GCT, and local
  holiday awareness.
- Keep the project structured enough for humans and AI tools to safely extend it.

## Current Application Capabilities

- Dashboard analytics and recent activity.
- Lead import and CRM contact management.
- Sales pipeline tracking.
- Call logs and call sync workflow.
- Task management.
- Quote and invoice workflows.
- Team and reporting views.
- Settings and documentation pages.
- WooCommerce, WhatsApp, email, Supabase, and Vercel API integration surfaces.

## Success Criteria

- Developers and AI agents can understand the app structure without scanning the
  whole repository.
- New work starts from project context, architecture, file map, task status, and
  known risks.
- Changes are documented in the same workflow that guided the implementation.
- The application continues to build successfully after workflow docs and tooling
  are added.

## Out Of Scope For This Workflow Setup

- Rebuilding authentication.
- Changing CRM business behavior.
- Moving runtime dependencies.
- Storing private credentials in documentation.
- Replacing existing app documentation.
